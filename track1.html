Lesson 1: What is Raiku?

Raiku is a coordination layer on top of Solana. Its goal is to make blockspace predictable, reliable, and programmable. Normally, congestion leads to failures and delays. With Raiku, transactions can reserve slots in advance through a marketplace run by validators via the Sidecar plugin.
